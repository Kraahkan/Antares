Services

Psychotherapy and Nature-Based Sessions

Remote Psychotherapy for Adults

Individual psychotherapy for adults throughout Canada, offered by secure video or telephone. Sessions provide a confidential, collaborative space to explore personal challenges, better understand the impact of life experiences, and work toward individual goals.

Nature-Based Sessions

Limited in-person nature-based sessions are available in the Sun Peaks area. These sessions incorporate the natural environment to support reflection, awareness and connection.

Consulting and research

Specialized Professional Services

Gladue and Specialized Report Writing

Preparation of comprehensive Gladue Reports, drawing on more than 13 years of experience researching and documenting the social, historical, cultural and personal circumstances relevant to Indigenous people involved in the criminal justice system.

Criminal and Civil Litigation Consulting

Comprehensive review and analysis of legal, clinical and medical disclosure materials. This includes psychological and psychiatric assessments, health records, social work documentation and related evidence. Identifying inconsistencies, evidentiary gaps and areas requiring further consideration, and preparing detailed written analyses in response to counsel's questions.

Mental Health and Diagnostic Research

Specialized research and analysis relating to mental health diagnoses, complex trauma, acquired brain injuries, fetal alcohol spectrum disorder, substance use and addictions. Providing evidence-informed information to assist legal counsel in understanding clinical documentation, diagnostic considerations and the relationship between reported conditions and available evidence.

Indigenous Justice and Psychosocial Context

Culturally responsive research and analysis addressing the historical, social, cultural and systemic factors that influence individual circumstances, particularly in the context of Indigenous justice involvement.

About

Karen Lara, MC, CCC

Karen Lara is a Canadian Certified Counsellor with a Master's degree in Counselling Psychology (Honours), a BA in Psychology (Honours), and more than 25 years of experience in human services.

Her professional experience spans clinical counselling, complex trauma, mental health, substance use and the criminal justice system. She has extensive experience working with individuals experiencing complex psychosocial circumstances, systemic barriers and involvement with the justice system.

In addition to her clinical practice, Karen has specialized experience in Indigenous justice, Gladue report writing, psychosocial research and professional report development.

Her approach is trauma-informed, collaborative and culturally responsive, recognizing the importance of individual strengths, lived experience, historical and systemic influences, and personal circumstances.

Karen is committed to working alongside individuals and organizations in ways that promote dignity, understanding, accountability, healing and meaningful change.

About Antares

Antares is an independent professional practice led by Karen Lara, MC, CCC. Grounded in clinical experience, research and a trauma-informed perspective, Antares provides psychotherapy and specialized professional services. The practice is guided by respect for individual experiences, an appreciation of the social and cultural contexts that shape people's lives, and a commitment to thoughtful, collaborative and evidence-informed work.
